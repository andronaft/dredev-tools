R-454B vs R-410A: pressures, glide and safety class

The published PT charts of R-454B and R-410A side by side at the same temperatures, with glide, safety class and GWP.

Quick answer: at the same temperatures R-454B runs at lower pressure than R-410A. As saturated vapor at 40 °F, R-454B is 107.0 psig against 118.8 psig (-11.8 psi). As saturated liquid at 105 °F, R-454B is 322.8 psig against 341.9 psig (-19.1 psi, about 6 % lower).

Checked 12 November 2026 by Andrii Zhukovskyi. Pressures are cells of the Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025); differences are computed from them.

Can R-454B replace R-410A?

R-454B is not a top-off or retrofit refrigerant for R-410A equipment. Chemours' R-454B guide says A2L refrigerants are for new equipment only (Chemours, Opteon™ XL41 (R-454B) Pressure-Temperature Guide for A/C), and Arkema lists R-454B as an R-410A replacement for new equipment (Arkema, 2025 Forane® Technical Digest). York's R-454B manual has the outdoor unit, indoor coil and metering device replaced together when an R-410A system is replaced (York YH9/XH9 R-454B heat pump Installation Manual 6515371-UIM-B-0526).

A changeover follows the refrigerant maker's and the equipment maker's instructions. This page only compares the published pressure data.

Questions

Is R-454B higher or lower pressure than R-410A?

At 105 °F saturated liquid R-454B is 322.8 psig and R-410A is 341.9 psig, a difference of -19.1 psi. At 40 °F saturated vapor it is 107.0 psig against 118.8 psig (-11.8 psi).

Can I use the R-410A PT chart for R-454B?

No. At 119 psig on the suction side the two charts give 45.5 °F for R-454B and 40.1 °F for R-410A, so superheat would be off by 5.4 °F. At 342 psig on the liquid line subcooling would be off by 4.2 °F. Use the chart for the refrigerant in the system.

Which has more temperature glide, R-454B or R-410A?

R-454B: 2.3 °F at a 40 °F dew point and 2.3 °F at a 105 °F bubble point (dew minus bubble temperature at the same pressure, computed from this chart). R-410A: the source chart prints one pressure column, used for both superheat and subcooling..

Can R-454B replace R-410A in an existing system?
