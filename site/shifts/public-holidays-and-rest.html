Rules for shift workers

Minimum rest between shifts and public holidays, by country

How long the law says you must rest between two shifts, where that rule is written, and the public holidays of 2027, for the countries the ShiftWise app covers.

Quick answer: 7 countries in this table and all three parts of the UK give at least 11 hours of rest between two working days; Spain gives 12. Ukraine asks for twice the length of the previous shift, and Mexico and US federal law set no minimum. Below: the law behind each rule and every public holiday in 2027.

Worked example: a late shift followed by an early

  1. The late shift ends at 22:00 and the next early starts at 06:00: the rest between them is 8 hours.
  2. That is 3 hours short of the 11-hour rule in the UK, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, Poland and Brazil, and 4 hours short of Spain's 12.
  3. In the Netherlands 8 hours is allowed once in every 7 days when the work requires it (Arbeidstijdenwet art. 5:3).
  4. Moving the early start to 09:00 gives 11 hours; Spain needs 10:00 for 12.

Result: a late-then-early swap is the most common way a rota breaks the daily rest rule; leave a day between them or start later.

Questions

How many hours of rest do you need between shifts in the UK?

At least 11 consecutive hours in each 24-hour period for workers 18 and over: regulation 10 of the Working Time Regulations 1998 in Great Britain, regulation 12 of the 2016 regulations in Northern Ireland. Young workers get 12, and the regulations list exceptions for some jobs.

Is there a minimum rest between shifts in the US?

Not in federal law: the Fair Labor Standards Act sets no limit on the hours of employees 16 and over and has no daily rest rule. Some states, sectors such as transport, and union contracts set their own.

Why does Spain need 12 hours?

The Estatuto de los Trabajadores, article 34.3, says at least twelve hours must pass between the end of one working day and the start of the next. The EU directive asks for 11; Spain gives more.

Can the 11 hours be shortened?

Each law has its own exceptions, written next to the rule: Germany allows 10 hours in hospitals, care, hotels, transport and a few other sectors if each cut is made up within a month or four weeks with a rest of at least 12 hours, and the Netherlands allows 8 hours once a week. Read the article linked in the table for your country.

Do public holidays count for shift workers?

Shifts go on, but whether a holiday is paid extra or gives a day off depends on your contract or collective agreement. In Ireland a holiday on a day you don't work gives you an entitlement instead, and in Ukraine, under martial law, they are ordinary working days paid at the single rate (Law 2136-IX, as ShiftWise applies it).
