R-409A PT Chart

R-409A pressure–temperature chart in psig and °F/°C. Refrigeration (R-12 retrofit).

R-409A is a zeotropic blend with temperature glide, so the chart has two columns: use the vapor (dew) column for superheat and the liquid (bubble) column for subcooling.

Vacuum readings are shown in inches of mercury (″ Hg) below atmospheric pressure.
