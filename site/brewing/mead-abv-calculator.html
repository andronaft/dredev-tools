Mead ABV calculator

Alcohol by volume for mead, cider and wine from the original and final gravity, in SG, Brix or Plato.

Quick answer: ABV = (OG − FG) × 131.25. A mead that starts at 1.100 and finishes at 1.010 is about 11.8 % ABV; a cider from 1.050 to 1.000 is 6.6 %. For Brix or Plato readings the calculator converts to specific gravity first.

Worked example: a mead from 1.100 down to 1.010

  1. Gravity drop: 1.100 − 1.010 = 0.090.
  2. Standard formula: 0.090 × 131.25 = 11.8 % ABV.
  3. Hall's formula, closer for strong meads and wines: 76.08 × 0.090 ÷ (1.775 − 1.100) × (1.010 ÷ 0.794) = 12.9 %.
  4. Apparent attenuation: 0.090 ÷ 0.100 = 90 %. A final gravity of 1.010 sits on the line between dry and semi-sweet.

Result: about 12 to 13 % ABV, a standard-strength mead.

Questions

How do you calculate the ABV of mead?

Take a hydrometer reading before you pitch the yeast (original gravity, OG) and another when fermentation is over (final gravity, FG). ABV = (OG − FG) × 131.25. From 1.100 to 1.000 that is 13.1 %.

Which ABV formula is right for strong mead and wine?

The standard × 131.25 formula reads low as the alcohol goes up. Michael Hall's formula, 76.08 × (OG − FG) ÷ (1.775 − OG) × (FG ÷ 0.794), is closer above about 10 %. The calculator shows both.

Can I use a refractometer for the final gravity?

Not directly. Alcohol bends light too, so a refractometer reads too high once fermentation has started. Use a hydrometer for the final reading, or a refractometer correction formula with both Brix readings (the Ferment app has one).

Are Brix and Plato the same?

For home brewing, close enough: both are percent sugar by weight and they agree to the first decimal at these gravities. This calculator uses SG = 1 + °P ÷ (258.6 − °P ÷ 258.2 × 227.1) for both.

Does it work for cider and wine?

Yes. The formula only needs the gravity drop, whatever the sugar came from: apple juice, grape must or honey.

What if I added honey or sugar during fermentation?

Step feeding raises the real original gravity. Add the gravity points of every addition to the OG before you calculate, or use the honey calculator to find the points each addition gave.
