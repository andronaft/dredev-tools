Yarn yardage calculator

How much yarn a scarf, hat or blanket needs, from a swatch in your own yarn, needles and stitch. Works for knitting and crochet.

Quick answer: knit a swatch in your stitch, weigh it, and scale by area. If a 4 × 4 in swatch takes 7 yd, an 8 × 60 in scarf needs about 211 yd and a 50 × 60 in throw about 1,320 yd, plus about 10 % extra. No yardage table knows your yarn, needles and stitch.

Yarn = swatch yarn × project area ÷ swatch area, before any extra. Sizes are examples: enter your own. The hat is counted as a tube, head circumference × height, so it comes out a little generous.

Worked example: a scarf from a swatch

  1. Your 4 × 4 in swatch weighs 3.2 g. The ball band says 100 g is 220 yd, so the swatch holds 3.2 × 220 ÷ 100 = 7.04 yd.
  2. The scarf is 8 × 60 in = 480 sq in, which is 480 ÷ 16 = 30 swatches: 30 × 7.04 = 211 yd.
  3. Add 10 % for the cast-on, ends and tension: 232 yd. One ball is 220 yd, so 232 ÷ 220 = 1.06, round up.

Result: about 232 yd (212 m), buy 2 balls from one dye lot.

Questions

How much yarn do I need for a scarf?

It depends on the yarn and the stitch, which is why a swatch beats a table. As an example, if your 4 × 4 in swatch takes 7 yd, a plain 8 × 60 in scarf needs about 211 yd, or 232 yd with 10 % extra.

How much yarn do I need for a blanket?

With the same 7 yd swatch, a 30 × 36 in baby blanket needs about 475 yd and a 50 × 60 in throw about 1,320 yd before the extra. Thicker yarn covers the same area with fewer yards.

How do I find the yards in my swatch?

Weigh the swatch on a kitchen scale. Yards in the swatch = swatch weight × yards on the ball band ÷ grams on the ball band. A 3.2 g swatch from a 100 g, 220 yd ball holds about 7 yd.

Does this work for crochet?

Yes. Crochet a swatch in the stitch you will use and weigh it. The calculator only scales your own swatch, so the stitch is already counted.

How much extra yarn should I buy?

About 10 % covers the cast-on, bind-off, ends and small tension changes. Add more for fringe, pompoms or a border, and buy it all from one dye lot.
