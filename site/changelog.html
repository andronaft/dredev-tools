What changed and when

Changelog

New calculators, changed formulas and corrected mistakes, newest first. A correction says what was wrong and for how long.

DateKindWhat changed
13 November 2026NewCalculators for roofing, concrete slabs, garage floor coatings, pool chlorine and shift patterns, and for mead and cider, hydroponics, candles, espresso and knitting. Each uses the formulas of the matching Dre Dev app. Some can be embedded on other sites.
13 November 2026NewThe superheat and subcooling calculator now covers all 22 refrigerants, and a link can open it on one of them (the PT chart pages do). R-410A, R-454B, R-32, R-22, R-134a and R-404A have a page of their own with worked examples and typical pressures.
13 November 2026NewPT chart pages show pressures at common temperatures, safety class, GWP and lubricant with their sources, and print cleanly. New: refrigerant comparisons, a target superheat calculator for fixed-orifice systems, guides on bubble and dew points and on charging R-454B by subcooling, an NEC 310.16 ampacity chart, wire size by distance and voltage drop references.
12 November 2026CorrectionLye calculator: the oil list and the SAP table were empty from 9 November 2026, so the page could not calculate anything. Fixed; the build now stops if the oil list is incomplete.
12 November 2026NewMethodology and sources, checked examples and this changelog. One shared disclaimer and a "Found an error?" link on every calculator page. The author line links to a page about who makes these tools.
12 November 2026NewCalculator pages got a quick answer, a chart of common cases and a worked example, computed with the same formulas as the live calculator.
9 November 2026ChangeThe site moved from andronaft.github.io/dredev-tools to zhukovskyi.space/calculators. Old addresses forward to the new ones.
26 October 2026NewFirst version: superheat and subcooling calculator, 22 refrigerant PT charts, voltage drop, wire size, AWG to mm², epoxy coverage, pool chemicals, baker's percentages, lye calculator and a Christmas countdown.

Found something wrong? Tell me: the page, what you entered, what it showed and the source you compared with. Confirmed mistakes are fixed and listed here.
