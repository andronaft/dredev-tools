Refrigerant PT data: CSV and JSON

The pressure-temperature tables behind the PT charts and the superheat calculator on this site, as two files: 22 refrigerants, 883 rows, one row every 5 °F. Free to use with credit.

Download CSV Download JSON

What is in the files

Saturation pressure at each temperature for 22 refrigerants. 9 of them are blends with two columns, liquid (bubble point) and vapor (dew point); the other 13 have one column. Both files hold the same rows. The JSON file also carries the column descriptions, the list of sources and one line per refrigerant.

Where the numbers come from

Nothing in the files was measured by me. 17 refrigerants are read from three published manufacturer tables; 5 are computed with the open-source CoolProp library. Each row names its source.

How the values got here:

  • The Arkema charts print gauge pressure (psig) every 5 °F, and inches of mercury below one atmosphere for vacuum cells. A script read both PDFs cell by cell; the values were copied, not recalculated. In the files the vacuum cells are converted to negative psi (1 inHg = 0.4911541 psi), so one column has one unit.
  • The Chemours table gives absolute pressure (psia). The 5 °F rows were kept and turned into gauge pressure by subtracting the standard atmosphere, 14.695949 psi, then rounded the way the printed charts are: to 0.1 psi above atmospheric pressure and to 0.1 inHg below it. The source itself gives more digits.
  • R-290, R-600a, R-744, R-717, R-12 are computed: saturation pressure of the pure fluid at each temperature with CoolProp 8.0.0 (PropsSI("P", "T", T, "Q", 0, fluid)), kept to 0.01 psi. The PT charts on this site for these five were read from the NIST Chemistry WebBook; the computed values agree with them within 0.04 psi, and the build stops if a row ever differs from its chart cell by more than rounding. The files carry the computed values so that every number in them is either a manufacturer's printed cell or the output of MIT-licensed software.
  • The absolute columns (psia, kpa) and temp_c are plain unit conversions of the columns next to them.
  • R-427A is taken from the 2019 Arkema chart, because the 2025 edition prints two cells of another refrigerant in its 150 °F row.
  • A few vapor cells of R-448A, R-449A and R-452A, and the rows of R-513A below −20 °F, are left out: the 2025 chart prints those vacuum cells in a unit that does not match its legend, so they were not re-interpreted. That is why some vapor_psig cells are empty and R-513A starts at −20 °F.

Checks, as listed in the methodology: Arkema's R-454B columns agree with Chemours' 1 °F table for Opteon™ XL41 within 0.06 psi, and values interpolated between rows agree with CoolProp 8.0 (NIST reference equations). No outside trade professional or laboratory has reviewed the files.

Limits

Reference only. Charging targets and procedures come from the equipment manufacturer's data plate and installation instructions, and local codes apply. Handling refrigerants requires EPA Section 608 certification in the US.

The tables stop where the printed charts stop; there is no extrapolation. Gauge pressure assumes sea level. Version 1.0.0, published 20 November 2026. Corrections are listed in the changelog. Found a wrong cell? Send the refrigerant, the temperature and the source you compared with.
