Add a Christmas countdown widget

  1. Install Days Until and open it.
  2. Tap + and pick the Christmas template (🎄). The date fills in by itself.
  3. Long-press your home screen, tap Widgets, find Days Until and drag it onto the screen.
  4. Tap the widget to choose the Christmas event. The number of days updates every day.

Questions

How many days until Christmas?

The counter at the top of this page shows the exact days, hours, minutes and seconds until 25 December in your own time zone, and updates live. After Christmas it switches to next year's date.

How many sleeps until Christmas?

Sleeps are the nights left before Christmas morning: on Christmas Eve there is one sleep left. The page shows them under the main counter.

How do I put a Christmas countdown on my home screen?

Install Days Until, tap + and pick the Christmas template, then long-press your home screen, choose Widgets and add the Days Until widget. The number of days updates by itself every day.

Can I count down to New Year too?

Yes. The page shows a New Year countdown below the Christmas one, and Days Until has a New Year template as well as birthdays, trips and weddings.
