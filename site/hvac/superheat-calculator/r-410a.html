R-410A Superheat & Subcooling Calculator

Enter gauge pressure and line temperature for R-410A. Saturation comes from the published R-410A PT chart.

Quick answer: with R-410A, superheat = suction line temperature − saturation temperature at the suction pressure, and subcooling = saturation temperature at the liquid pressure − liquid line temperature. Example: 119 psig on the suction side is 40.1 °F saturated, so a 52 °F suction line has 11.9 °F of superheat.

R-410A worked examples and saturation table

The readings below are a comfort-cooling example. They show the arithmetic; they are not charging targets.

Worked example: R-410A superheat

  1. Suction (low-side) gauge pressure: 119 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 119 psig in the chart: 40.1 °F.
  3. Suction line temperature near the service valve: 52 °F.
  4. Superheat = 52 − 40.1 = 11.9 °F.

Result: 11.9 °F of superheat.

Worked example: R-410A subcooling

  1. Liquid (high-side) gauge pressure: 366 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 366 psig in the chart: 109.9 °F.
  3. Liquid line temperature near the service valve: 100 °F.
  4. Subcooling = 109.9 − 100 = 9.9 °F.

Result: 9.9 °F of subcooling.

What should R-410A superheat and subcooling be?

There is no single correct number for a refrigerant. The target belongs to the equipment: a fixed-orifice (piston) system is charged to a target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table (target superheat calculator), and a TXV or EEV system to the target subcooling on the unit's data plate. Always use the manufacturer's numbers for the unit in front of you.

Questions

How do I calculate superheat for R-410A?

Convert the suction pressure to the saturation temperature with the R-410A PT chart and subtract it from the suction line temperature. Example: 119 psig is 40.1 °F, and a 52 °F line gives 11.9 °F of superheat.

How do I calculate subcooling for R-410A?

Convert the liquid pressure to the saturation temperature and subtract the liquid line temperature. Example: 366 psig is 109.9 °F, and a 100 °F liquid line gives 9.9 °F of subcooling.

What is the saturation temperature of R-410A at 119 psig?

40.1 °F (4.5 °C).

What superheat or subcooling should a R-410A system have?

It depends on the equipment, not on the refrigerant alone. Use the target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table for a fixed-orifice system, or the target subcooling on the data plate for a TXV or EEV system.

Where does the data come from?

Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025). Between the printed 5 °F points the calculator interpolates along the vapor-pressure curve.
