Embed this calculator

Put one of our calculators on your own site with a short piece of code. Free, no sign-up, no ads inside the frame.

How to add it

  1. Pick a calculator below and copy its code.
  2. Paste it into your page where the calculator should appear (an HTML or code block in your site builder).
  3. Publish. If it looks cut off, change the height number.

The code has two parts: the calculator itself and one line under it, "Calculator: Dre Dev Tools", that links to the full page with the charts and sources. Please keep both as they are. The link carries rel="nofollow", so it passes no ranking credit and is safe under search engines' rules for widget links.

Terms, in plain words

  • Free for any site, a shop or a paid course included. No sign-up, no key, no limit on page views.
  • Keep the "Calculator: Dre Dev Tools" line with its link under the frame. That line is the only thing asked in return.
  • Do not change the calculator, hide the "Powered by" line or present it as your own tool.
  • The calculators are reference tools: the limits on the methodology page apply on your site as well.
  • A calculator may be updated when a number is corrected; corrections are listed in the changelog. If one ever has to be withdrawn, its address will keep answering with a link to the full page.

The superheat calculator can open on one refrigerant: add it after a # sign in the frame address, for example superheat-subcooling.html#r-454b.

Questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Free, with no sign-up and no key. Copy the code and paste it into your page.

What does the embedded calculator show?

Only the calculator, with a small "Powered by Dre Dev Tools" line, and under the frame one line of text with a link to the full page with the charts and sources. No ads, no cookies and no analytics inside the frame.

Can I change the size?

Yes. The width follows your page up to 720 px. If the calculator is cut off or leaves a gap on your layout, change the height number in the code.

Will the numbers stay up to date?

Yes. The frame loads the calculator from this site, so fixes and updates reach your page without any change on your side.

Which platforms does it work on?

Anywhere you can paste HTML: WordPress (Custom HTML block), Squarespace (Code block), Wix (Embed HTML), Shopify pages and plain HTML sites.
