Shift pattern calendars

Six common rotas day by day, with a calendar generator for your own start date, the average week and the days off each one gives.

Quick answer: 4 on 4 off, 2-2-3 (Pitman), DuPont and Continental are four-crew rotas that average 42 hours a week; 3 on 3 off also averages 42, and 4 on 3 off is a 40-hour week of four 10-hour days. Each page below has a calendar generator and the days off it adds up to.

Worked example: why 12-hour patterns average 42 hours

  1. A year has 8,766 hours on average (365.25 × 24). Four crews covering all of them work 8,766 ÷ 4 = 2,191.5 hours each.
  2. Per week: 2,191.5 ÷ 52.18 weeks = 42 hours.
  3. So every four-crew rota that covers 24 hours a day lands on 42 hours a week, whether the shifts are 8 or 12 hours long. Only the rhythm of work and rest differs.

Result: 4 on 4 off, 2-2-3, DuPont and Continental all average 42 hours a week.

Questions

Which shift pattern gives the most days off?

Counted in days, the 12-hour patterns: 4 on 4 off, 2-2-3, DuPont and 3 on 3 off all give about 183 days off a year, because you work half the days. The price is longer shifts.

Which pattern has the longest break?

DuPont: seven days off in a row once every 28 days. It also has a 72-hour week in the cycle.

What is the difference between Pitman and 2-2-3?

They are the same sequence: 2 on, 2 off, 3 on, 2 off, 2 on, 3 off over 14 days. "Panama" is another name used for it.

Can I put my rota on my phone?

Yes. ShiftWise has 4 on 4 off, Pitman, DuPont and 3 on 3 off as presets and lets you build any other rota day by day. It shows your shifts in a calendar and a home screen widget and exports them to your phone's calendar.
