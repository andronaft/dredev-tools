R-454B PT Chart

R-454B pressure–temperature chart in psig and °F/°C. New AC and heat pumps (R-410A replacement). It is an A2L (mildly flammable) refrigerant: follow A2L handling and equipment rules.

R-454B is a zeotropic blend with temperature glide, so the chart has two columns: use the vapor (dew) column for superheat and the liquid (bubble) column for subcooling.

Vacuum readings are shown in inches of mercury (″ Hg) below atmospheric pressure.
