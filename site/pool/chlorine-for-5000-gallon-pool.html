How much chlorine for a 5,000 gallon pool

Liquid chlorine, bleach, cal-hypo, dichlor and trichlor for a 5,000 gallon pool, by the ppm of free chlorine you need to add.

Quick answer: each 1 ppm of free chlorine in a 5,000 gallon pool takes about 6.4 fl oz of 10 % liquid chlorine, 1.0 oz of 65 % cal-hypo or 0.7 oz of 90 % trichlor. Raising it from 2 to 5 ppm takes 19.2 fl oz of 10 % liquid chlorine. Follow the product label.

Before you add chlorine

  • The product label comes first. Read it and follow its directions and dose limits, even where this page says something different.
  • Never mix pool chemicals with each other, and never mix different kinds of chlorine. Mixing can release toxic gas or start a fire. Use a clean, dry scoop for each product.
  • Wear goggles and gloves, work outdoors or with good ventilation, and keep children and pets away.
  • Add one chemical at a time to the pool water with the pump running. Never add water to a chemical.
  • Store chemicals in their original labelled containers, dry, closed and apart from each other.
  • Add in portions, let the water circulate, test again, and wait until the chlorine is back in its normal range before anyone swims.

More on storing and handling pool chemicals: CDC, pool chemical safety (read 12 November 2026). If someone breathes fumes or gets chemical in the eyes, call emergency services or poison control.

Worked example: 5,000 gallons, free chlorine from 2 to 5 ppm

  1. Rise needed: 5 − 2 = 3 ppm.
  2. 5,000 gallons is 18,927 litres, so 3 ppm is 3 × 18,927 ÷ 1,000 = 57 g of chlorine.
  3. 10 % liquid chlorine holds 100 g per litre: 57 ÷ 100 = 0.57 L, which is 19 fl oz.
  4. With 65 % cal-hypo instead: 3.1 oz.

Result: about 19 fl oz (0.15 gal) of 10 % liquid chlorine.

Questions

How much liquid chlorine for a 5,000 gallon pool?

6.4 fl oz of 10 % liquid chlorine for each 1 ppm, so 19.2 fl oz for a 3 ppm rise. With 12.5 % it is 5.1 fl oz per ppm.

How much does a gallon of liquid chlorine raise a 5,000 gallon pool?

One gallon of 10 % liquid chlorine adds about 20.0 ppm of free chlorine; one gallon of 12.5 % adds 25.0 ppm.

How much cal-hypo for a 5,000 gallon pool?

1.0 oz of 65 % cal-hypo per 1 ppm of free chlorine, 5.1 oz for 5 ppm. It adds calcium hardness too, so watch that level in hard-water areas.

How many pounds of trichlor does a 5,000 gallon pool use?

One pound of 90 % trichlor adds 21.6 ppm of free chlorine and 13.1 ppm of stabilizer here. How much the pool uses per week depends on sun, heat and swimmers, so test and adjust.

Is it safe to add this much at once?

Read the label for the largest single dose, add in portions with the pump running and test again before adding more. Never mix products.
