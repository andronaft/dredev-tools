R-452A PT Chart

R-452A pressure–temperature chart in psig and °F/°C. Transport refrigeration (R-404A replacement).

Quick answer: at 95 °F, R-452A is 230.4 psig as saturated liquid (bubble point) and 209.9 psig as saturated vapor (dew point); at 40 °F it is 91.5 psig and 79.4 psig. Use the vapor (dew) column for superheat and the liquid (bubble) column for subcooling.

Checked 12 November 2026 by Andrii Zhukovskyi. Every pressure on this page is a cell of the Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025).

Questions

What is the pressure of R-452A at 95 °F?

230.4 psig as saturated liquid (bubble point) and 209.9 psig as saturated vapor (dew point), from the Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025).

What is the pressure of R-452A at 40 °F?

91.5 psig liquid (bubble) and 79.4 psig vapor (dew).

How do I read the bubble and dew columns for R-452A?

A blend boils over a range of temperatures at one pressure. The bubble point is where the liquid starts to boil and the dew point is where the last liquid has boiled off. Read the suction pressure in the vapor (dew) column to get the saturation temperature for superheat, and the liquid-line pressure in the liquid (bubble) column for subcooling. For R-452A the two differ by about 7.0 °F at a 40 °F dew point. More on bubble and dew points.

Can I print this chart?

Yes. Use the Print button: the page prints as a clean table without the menus, with the source and the check date at the bottom.

Where does the data come from?

Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025), copied cell for cell every 5 °F. The calculator interpolates between the printed points along the vapor-pressure curve.
