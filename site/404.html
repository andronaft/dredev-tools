Why these tools

No account, no ads, no data leaving your phone. Built by an independent developer, Andrii Zhukovskyi, who answers every email: mobile.support@zhukovskyi.space.

Every table and formula names its source. The methodology lists the editions and the limits, and the checked examples put a row from each source next to what the calculator answers.

Most of the apps are in a free closed test on Google Play right now, with the App Store next. Become a tester and get them before release.
