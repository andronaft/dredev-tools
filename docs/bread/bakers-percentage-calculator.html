Questions

What are baker's percentages?

Every ingredient is written as a percentage of the flour weight, with flour at 100 %. 500 g flour at 75 % hydration means 375 g water.

What is true hydration?

The water from both the bowl and the starter divided by all the flour, including the flour in the starter. A 75 % dough with 20 % starter at 100 % hydration is about 75 % either way; stiff starters lower it.

How much starter should I use?

Most sourdough recipes use 10–25 % starter. Less starter means a longer, slower rise.
