Water Buddy: Drink Reminder

A cute water tracker: fill up Buddy, hit your daily goal, unlock outfits and keep your streak.

Get early accessApp Store & Google Play: coming soon

Drinking enough water is easier with a friend. Buddy is a little water drop who fills up every time you drink and gets happier as you go. Log a glass in one tap, hit your goal and celebrate with confetti.

Free
  • One-tap logging with a glass, a bottle or any amount
  • Daily goal based on your weight and activity
  • Gentle reminders, only while you are awake
  • Streaks, weekly chart, light and dark themes
Pro
  • 30-day history and insights
  • Your own reminder schedule
  • Custom cup sizes
  • Every Buddy outfit, unlocked
