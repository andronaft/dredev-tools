AWG to mm² converter

American wire gauge and kcmil to square millimetres and back, with the next standard IEC size.

Quick answer: 14 AWG = 2.08 mm², 12 AWG = 3.31 mm², 10 AWG = 5.26 mm², 8 AWG = 8.37 mm² and 6 AWG = 13.3 mm². 1 circular mil = 0.000507 mm². When you swap metric cable for AWG wire, pick the size that is not smaller.

Questions

Is 2.5 mm² the same as 14 AWG?

Close but not equal: 14 AWG is 2.08 mm² and 12 AWG is 3.31 mm². When you replace a metric cable with AWG wire, pick the size that is not smaller and check ampacity and voltage drop for your circuit.

Where do these numbers come from?

Areas are from NEC Chapter 9, Table 8 (circular mils), converted with 1 cmil = 0.000506707 mm². Metric sizes are the IEC 60228 series.

What is 6 mm² in AWG?

6 mm² sits between 10 AWG (5.26 mm²) and 8 AWG (8.37 mm²). The closest AWG that is not smaller is 8 AWG.

What is 4 AWG in mm²?

4 AWG is 41,740 circular mils, which is 21.2 mm². The next IEC size up is 25 mm².

Reference only. A result depends on what you enter and on the published tables named on this page.
