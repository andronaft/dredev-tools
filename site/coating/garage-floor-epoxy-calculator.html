Garage floor epoxy calculator

How many gallons and kits a one, two or three car garage needs, by coating system, thickness and solids.

Quick answer: with a 100 % solids epoxy at 10 mils and 10 % waste, a one-car garage (240 sq ft) takes about 1.6 gallons, a two-car (440 sq ft) about 3.0 and a three-car (650 sq ft) about 4.5. Thin water-based coats need far less per coat. Measure your floor: garages vary.

Worked example: a one-car garage, 240 sq ft, two thin coats of water-based epoxy

  1. One coat at 50 % solids and 3 mils: 240 × 3 ÷ (1,604 × 0.50) = 0.90 gal, plus 10 % = 0.99 gal.
  2. Two coats: 2 × 0.99 = 1.98 gal. In 1-gallon kits: 2.
  3. Dry film left on the floor: 2 × 3 = 6 mils, against 10 mils from one coat of 100 % solids epoxy.

Result: about 2.0 gal for two coats, 2 kits of 1 gal.

Worked example: a two-car garage, 440 sq ft, flake system

  1. Base coat, 100 % solids epoxy at 10 mils: 440 × 10 ÷ 1,604 = 2.74 gal, plus 10 % = 3.02 gal. In 3-gallon kits: 2 kits.
  2. Topcoat, polyaspartic at 87 % solids and 8 mils: 440 × 8 ÷ (1,604 × 0.87) × 1.10 = 2.77 gal. In 2-gallon kits: 2.
  3. Flake, primer and a second topcoat are sized the same way, each from its own data sheet.

Result: about 3.0 gal of epoxy base and 2.8 gal of polyaspartic topcoat.

Worked example: a three-car garage, 650 sq ft, polyurea base and polyaspartic topcoat

  1. Base coat, polyurea at 99 % solids and 7 mils: 650 × 7 ÷ (1,604 × 0.99) × 1.10 = 3.15 gal. In 2-gallon kits: 2.
  2. Topcoat, polyaspartic at 87 % solids and 8 mils: 650 × 8 ÷ (1,604 × 0.87) × 1.10 = 4.10 gal. In 2-gallon kits: 3.
  3. Kits round up: 2 kits of base hold 4 gal for the 3.2 gal needed.

Result: about 3.2 gal of polyurea base and 4.1 gal of polyaspartic topcoat.

Questions

How much epoxy do I need for a 2 car garage?

For 440 sq ft with a 100 % solids epoxy at 10 mils and 10 % waste: 3.0 gallons for the base coat. A 500 sq ft floor takes 3.4 gallons the same way.

How much epoxy for a 1 car garage?

About 1.6 gallons of 100 % solids epoxy at 10 mils for 240 sq ft, with 10 % waste.

How much epoxy for a 3 car garage?

About 4.5 gallons of 100 % solids epoxy at 10 mils for 650 sq ft, with 10 % waste. In 3-gallon kits that is 2 kits.

How many square feet does a gallon of epoxy cover?

1,604 sq ft at 1 mil, times the solids by volume, divided by the dry thickness in mils. A 100 % solids epoxy at 10 mils covers about 160 sq ft; a 50 % solids water-based epoxy at 3 mils covers about 267 sq ft, but leaves a much thinner film.

Why does a water-based kit cover more than a 100 % solids kit?

Because it goes on thinner and half of it evaporates. Coverage alone does not compare products: compare the dry film thickness you end up with.
