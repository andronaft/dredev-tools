Concrete slab calculator

Cubic yards, bags and rebar for a slab of any size, with waste and the ready-mix order rounded to the quarter yard.

Quick answer: cubic yards = length × width × thickness in feet ÷ 27. A 10 × 10 ft slab 4 in thick is 1.23 yards, a 20 × 20 is 4.94 and a 30 × 40 is 14.81, before waste. One cubic yard is 45 bags of 80 lb, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb.

Worked example: a 12 × 12 ft patio slab, 4 in thick, from bags or a truck

  1. Volume: 12 × 12 × (4 ÷ 12) = 48 cu ft, or 48 ÷ 27 = 1.78 cu yd.
  2. With 10 % extra: 52.8 cu ft = 1.96 cu yd. Ready-mix order: 2 cu yd.
  3. In bags: 52.8 ÷ 0.60 = 88 bags of 80 lb, or 118 bags of 60 lb.

Result: 2 cu yd of ready-mix, or 88 bags of 80 lb.

Worked example: a 24 × 24 ft slab, 4 in thick, with a rebar grid

  1. Area: 24 × 24 = 576 sq ft. Perimeter for the forms: 2 × (24 + 24) = 96 ft.
  2. Volume: 576 sq ft × 4 in ÷ 12 = 192.0 cu ft, ÷ 27 = 7.11 cu yd.
  3. With 10 % extra for uneven ground and spillage: 7.82 cu yd. Ready-mix is ordered by the quarter yard: 8 cu yd.
  4. In bags that would be 211.2 cu ft ÷ 0.60 = 352 bags of 80 lb, or ÷ 0.45 = 470 bags of 60 lb, so ready-mix is usually less work on a slab this size.
  5. Rebar at 16 in on centre: 19 bars of 23.5 ft and 19 bars of 23.5 ft, cut from 52 sticks of 20 ft #4, 639 lb of steel.

Result: order 8 cu yd of ready-mix and 52 sticks of #4 rebar.

Questions

How do I calculate concrete for a slab?

Multiply length × width × thickness, all in feet (4 in is 0.333 ft), to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 for cubic yards. Add 5–10 % for waste.

How many yards of concrete do I need for a 20x20 slab?

4.94 cubic yards at 4 in thick before waste, 5.43 with 10 % extra, so the ready-mix order is 5.5 yards. At 6 in thick it is 8.15 yards with the extra. The table has 30 sizes, and each one loads into the calculator.

How many 80 lb bags of concrete make a yard?

45. An 80 lb bag yields about 0.60 cu ft and a cubic yard is 27 cu ft. It takes 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb.

How many square feet does a yard of concrete cover?

81 sq ft at 4 in thick, 65 sq ft at 5 in and 54 sq ft at 6 in: 27 cu ft divided by the thickness in feet.

When should I order ready-mix instead of bags?

Around one cubic yard. Below that a truck is a short load with an extra fee; above it you would be mixing 45 or more 80 lb bags, and ready-mix is usually less work.

How much rebar does a slab need?

Count the bars each way: the spaces rounded up, plus one, kept 3 in clear of the edges. A 20 × 20 ft slab with #4 bar at 16 in on centre takes 32 sticks of 20 ft; at 24 in it takes 22. Bar size and spacing come from your plans or engineer.

How thick should a slab be?

That depends on what it carries and on the ground under it, so take it from your plans, your engineer or the local building department. The calculator and the tables price 4, 5, 6 and 8 in so you can compare.

Does the calculator handle footings, columns and stairs?

This page is for rectangular slabs. The SlabCalc app does footings, pads, columns, sonotubes, post holes, walls and stairs, and adds mesh and forms.
