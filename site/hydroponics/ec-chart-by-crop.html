Worked example: checking a lettuce reservoir

  1. The published range for lettuce is 1.2–1.8 mS/cm at pH 6.0–7.0.
  2. Your meter shows 1,050 ppm and is on the 700 scale: 1,050 ÷ 700 = 1.5 mS/cm. Inside the range.
  3. The same 1,050 ppm on a 500-scale meter would be 2.1 mS/cm: above the range, so you would add plain water.

Result: know your meter's scale before you compare a ppm reading with a chart.

Questions

What EC is best for hydroponic lettuce?

1.2–1.8 mS/cm, which is 600–900 ppm on the 500 scale or 840–1,260 ppm on the 700 scale, at pH 6.0–7.0 (Oklahoma State University Extension, HLA-6722). Start young plants at the low end.

What EC is best for hydroponic tomato?

2.0–4.0 mS/cm, which is 1,000–2,000 ppm on the 500 scale or 1,400–2,800 ppm on the 700 scale, at pH 6.0–6.5 (Oklahoma State University Extension, HLA-6722). Start young plants at the low end.

What EC is best for hydroponic basil?

1.0–1.6 mS/cm, which is 500–800 ppm on the 500 scale or 700–1,120 ppm on the 700 scale, at pH 5.5–6.0 (Oklahoma State University Extension, HLA-6722). Start young plants at the low end.

What EC is best for hydroponic strawberry?

1.8–2.2 mS/cm, which is 900–1,100 ppm on the 500 scale or 1,260–1,540 ppm on the 700 scale, at pH 6.0 (Oklahoma State University Extension, HLA-6722). Start young plants at the low end.

What EC is best for hydroponic cucumber?

1.7–2.0 mS/cm, which is 850–1,000 ppm on the 500 scale or 1,190–1,400 ppm on the 700 scale, at pH 5.0–5.5 (Oklahoma State University Extension, HLA-6722). Start young plants at the low end.

My EC is too high or too low. What do I do?

Too high: add plain water. Too low: add nutrients at your label's ratio. Then stir, wait a few minutes and measure again. GrowLab works out the exact litres of water or millilitres of each part from your reservoir size.

Are these ranges right for every variety and system?

They are starting points from one university fact sheet. Water quality, light, temperature and the variety all move the best value, so watch the plants and follow your nutrient maker's label.
