R-1234yf PT Chart

R-1234yf pressure–temperature chart in psig and °F/°C. Vehicle AC and some chillers (R-134a replacement). It is an A2L (mildly flammable) refrigerant: follow A2L handling and equipment rules.

Quick answer: at 95 °F, the saturation pressure of R-1234yf is 115.1 psig; at 40 °F it is 38.4 psig. The chart has one pressure column, used for both superheat and subcooling.

Checked 12 November 2026 by Andrii Zhukovskyi. Every pressure on this page is a cell of the Chemours, Thermodynamic Properties of Opteon™ YF, Engineering (I/P) Units.

Questions

What is the pressure of R-1234yf at 95 °F?

115.1 psig saturation pressure, from the Chemours, Thermodynamic Properties of Opteon™ YF, Engineering (I/P) Units.

What is the pressure of R-1234yf at 40 °F?

38.4 psig.

Why does the R-1234yf chart have one pressure column?

R-1234yf is a single-component refrigerant, so it boils and condenses at one temperature for each pressure.

Why are some readings in inches of mercury?

Below atmospheric pressure a gauge reads vacuum. The chart prints those cells as inches of mercury (″ Hg) below one atmosphere instead of psig.

Can I print this chart?

Yes. Use the Print button: the page prints as a clean table without the menus, with the source and the check date at the bottom.

Where does the data come from?

Chemours, Thermodynamic Properties of Opteon™ YF, Engineering (I/P) Units, copied cell for cell every 5 °F. Converted from absolute pressure (psia) to psig. The calculator interpolates between the printed points along the vapor-pressure curve.
