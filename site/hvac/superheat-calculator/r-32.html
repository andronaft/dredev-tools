Quick answer: with R-32, superheat = suction line temperature − saturation temperature at the suction pressure, and subcooling = saturation temperature at the liquid pressure − liquid line temperature. R-32 worked examples and saturation table

The readings below are a comfort-cooling example. They show the arithmetic; they are not charging targets.

Worked example: R-32 superheat

  1. Suction (low-side) gauge pressure: 121 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 121 psig in the chart: 40.0 °F.
  3. Suction line temperature near the service valve: 52 °F.
  4. Superheat = 52 − 40.0 = 12.0 °F.

Result: 12.0 °F of superheat.

Worked example: R-32 subcooling

  1. Liquid (high-side) gauge pressure: 375 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 375 psig in the chart: 110.0 °F.
  3. Liquid line temperature near the service valve: 100 °F.
  4. Subcooling = 110.0 − 100 = 10.0 °F.

Result: 10.0 °F of subcooling.

What should R-32 superheat and subcooling be?

There is no single correct number for a refrigerant. The target belongs to the equipment: a fixed-orifice (piston) system is charged to a target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table (target superheat calculator), and a TXV or EEV system to the target subcooling on the unit's data plate. Always use the manufacturer's numbers for the unit in front of you.

It is an A2L (mildly flammable) refrigerant: follow A2L handling and equipment rules.

Questions

How do I calculate superheat for R-32?

Convert the suction pressure to the saturation temperature with the R-32 PT chart and subtract it from the suction line temperature. Example: 121 psig is 40.0 °F, and a 52 °F line gives 12.0 °F of superheat.

How do I calculate subcooling for R-32?

Convert the liquid pressure to the saturation temperature and subtract the liquid line temperature. Example: 375 psig is 110.0 °F, and a 100 °F liquid line gives 10.0 °F of subcooling.

What is the saturation temperature of R-32 at 121 psig?

40.0 °F (4.4 °C).

What superheat or subcooling should a R-32 system have?

It depends on the equipment, not on the refrigerant alone. Use the target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table for a fixed-orifice system, or the target subcooling on the data plate for a TXV or EEV system.

Where does the data come from?

Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025). Between the printed 5 °F points the calculator interpolates along the vapor-pressure curve.
