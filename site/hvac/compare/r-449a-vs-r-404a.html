R-449A vs R-404A: pressures, glide and safety class

The published PT charts of R-449A and R-404A side by side at the same temperatures, with glide, safety class and GWP.

Quick answer: at the same temperatures R-449A runs at pressures close to, but not the same as, R-404A. As saturated vapor at 40 °F, R-449A is 71.8 psig against 86.9 psig (-15.1 psi). As saturated liquid at 105 °F, R-449A is 257.8 psig against 254.2 psig (+3.6 psi, about 1 % higher).

Checked 12 November 2026 by Andrii Zhukovskyi. Pressures are cells of the Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025); differences are computed from them.

Can R-449A replace R-404A?

Arkema describes R-449A as designed to replace R-404A, R-507A, R-22 and the R-407 series, typically with POE oil (Arkema, 2025 Forane® Technical Digest).

A changeover follows the refrigerant maker's and the equipment maker's instructions. This page only compares the published pressure data.

Questions

Is R-449A higher or lower pressure than R-404A?

At 105 °F saturated liquid R-449A is 257.8 psig and R-404A is 254.2 psig, a difference of +3.6 psi. At 40 °F saturated vapor it is 71.8 psig against 86.9 psig (-15.1 psi).

Can I use the R-404A PT chart for R-449A?

No. At 87 psig on the suction side the two charts give 49.0 °F for R-449A and 40.1 °F for R-404A, so superheat would be off by 9.0 °F. At 254 psig on the liquid line subcooling would be off by 1.0 °F. Use the chart for the refrigerant in the system.

Which has more temperature glide, R-449A or R-404A?

R-449A: 9.4 °F at a 40 °F dew point and 7.7 °F at a 105 °F bubble point (dew minus bubble temperature at the same pressure, computed from this chart). R-404A: the source chart prints one pressure column, used for both superheat and subcooling..

Can R-449A replace R-404A in an existing system?

Arkema describes R-449A as designed to replace R-404A, R-507A, R-22 and the R-407 series, typically with POE oil (Arkema, 2025 Forane® Technical Digest). A changeover follows the refrigerant maker's and the equipment maker's instructions; this page only compares published pressure data.
