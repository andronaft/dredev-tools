Roof pitch multiplier chart

Slope factors for every pitch from 1/12 to 12/12, and a calculator that turns a footprint into roof area, squares and bundles.

Quick answer: multiply the footprint by the slope factor: 1.054 for 4/12, 1.118 for 6/12, 1.202 for 8/12, 1.302 for 10/12 and 1.414 for 12/12. A 2,000 sq ft footprint at 6/12 is 2,236 sq ft of roof, or 22.4 squares.

Worked example: a 50 × 40 ft footprint at 6/12

  1. Footprint with overhangs: 50 × 40 = 2,000 sq ft.
  2. Slope factor for 6/12: √(144 + 36) ÷ 12 = 1.118. Roof area: 2,000 × 1.118 = 2,236 sq ft.
  3. Squares: 2,236 ÷ 100 = 22.4. With 10 % waste at 3 bundles per square: 74 bundles.
  4. A hip on the same roof runs 1.5 ft for every foot of run, so a hip over a 20 ft run is 30 ft long.

Result: 2,236 sq ft of roof, 22.4 squares, 74 bundles of shingles.

Questions

What is the roof pitch multiplier for a 6/12 roof?

1.118. Multiply the footprint area or the horizontal run by it to get the sloped area or the rafter length. The angle is 26.6°.

How do I calculate a roof pitch multiplier?

Slope factor = √(12² + rise²) ÷ 12. For 8/12: √(144 + 64) ÷ 12 = 1.202.

What is the hip and valley factor?

The length of a hip or valley per foot of common run when both planes have the same pitch: √(288 + rise²) ÷ 12. For 6/12 it is 1.500, for 12/12 1.732.

Does the multiplier work for hip roofs?

Yes, for the area. As long as every plane has the same pitch, roof area = footprint × slope factor, whether the roof is a gable or a hip. Hips and cut-up roofs need more waste for the cuts.

How do I measure the pitch?

Hold a level horizontally against the roof or a rafter, mark 12 in along it and measure straight down to the roof. That drop in inches is the rise per 12.
