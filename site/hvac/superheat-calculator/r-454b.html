R-454B Superheat & Subcooling Calculator

Enter gauge pressure and line temperature for R-454B. Saturation comes from the published R-454B PT chart, with the dew point for superheat and the bubble point for subcooling.

Quick answer: with R-454B, superheat = suction line temperature − dew point at the suction pressure, and subcooling = bubble point at the liquid pressure − liquid line temperature. Example: 107 psig on the suction side is 40.0 °F saturated vapor, so a 52 °F suction line has 12.0 °F of superheat.

The readings below are a comfort-cooling example. They show the arithmetic; they are not charging targets.

Worked example: R-454B superheat

  1. Suction (low-side) gauge pressure: 107 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 107 psig in the vapor (dew) column: 40.0 °F.
  3. Suction line temperature near the service valve: 52 °F.
  4. Superheat = 52 − 40.0 = 12.0 °F.

Result: 12.0 °F of superheat.

Worked example: R-454B subcooling

  1. Liquid (high-side) gauge pressure: 346 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 346 psig in the liquid (bubble) column: 110.0 °F.
  3. Liquid line temperature near the service valve: 100 °F.
  4. Subcooling = 110.0 − 100 = 10.0 °F.

Result: 10.0 °F of subcooling.

What should R-454B superheat and subcooling be?

There is no single correct number for a refrigerant. The target belongs to the equipment: a fixed-orifice (piston) system is charged to a target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table (target superheat calculator), and a TXV or EEV system to the target subcooling on the unit's data plate. Always use the manufacturer's numbers for the unit in front of you.

It is an A2L (mildly flammable) refrigerant: follow A2L handling and equipment rules.

Questions

How do I calculate superheat for R-454B?

Convert the suction pressure to the dew point (vapor column) with the R-454B PT chart and subtract it from the suction line temperature. Example: 107 psig is 40.0 °F, and a 52 °F line gives 12.0 °F of superheat.

How do I calculate subcooling for R-454B?

Convert the liquid pressure to the bubble point (liquid column) and subtract the liquid line temperature. Example: 346 psig is 110.0 °F, and a 100 °F liquid line gives 10.0 °F of subcooling.

What is the saturation temperature of R-454B at 107 psig?

40.0 °F dew point (vapor) and 37.7 °F bubble point (liquid).

Do I use the bubble or the dew column for R-454B?

Dew (vapor) for superheat, bubble (liquid) for subcooling. R-454B is a blend with glide: 2.3 °F at a 40 °F dew point and 2.3 °F at a 105 °F bubble point. Using the wrong column shifts the result by that much.

What superheat or subcooling should a R-454B system have?

It depends on the equipment, not on the refrigerant alone. Use the target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table for a fixed-orifice system, or the target subcooling on the data plate for a TXV or EEV system.

Where does the data come from?

Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025). Between the printed 5 °F points the calculator interpolates along the vapor-pressure curve.
