3 on 3 off shift pattern

The 3 on 3 off rota day by day, a calendar from your own start date, and the hours and days off it adds up to.

Quick answer: Three 12-hour shifts, then three days off, repeating every six days. That is 3 shifts in a 6-day cycle: 42.0 hours a week on average with 12-hour shifts, and about 183 days off a year. Pick a date below to see your own calendar.

Worked example: average hours per week on 3 on 3 off

  1. One cycle is 6 days with 3 shifts of 12 hours: 3 × 12 = 36 hours.
  2. Per week: 36 ÷ 6 × 7 = 42.0 hours.
  3. Days off: 3 of every 6 days, which is 3 ÷ 6 × 365.25 = 183 days off in an average year.
  4. The longest stretch of work is 3 shifts in a row; the longest break is 3 days.

Result: 42.0 hours a week on average and about 183 days off a year.

Questions

How many hours a week is a 3 on 3 off shift pattern?

42.0 hours on average with 12-hour shifts: 3 shifts × 12 hours ÷ 6 days × 7. Single weeks are longer or shorter than the average.

How many days off a year do you get on 3 on 3 off?

About 183: 3 days off in every 6-day cycle. In 2027 the exact count is between 182 and 183, depending on the date your cycle starts. Annual leave comes on top.

How many weekends off do you get on 3 on 3 off?

In 2027, between 17 and 18 full weekends (Saturday and Sunday both off), depending on where your cycle starts.

How do I find where I am in the cycle?

Pick any date you know, for example today, and choose which day of the cycle it is: "Day 1" is the first shift of the block. The calendar fills in from there.

Does the calendar show my pay?

No, only the shifts and hours. The ShiftWise app adds your hourly rate, night and weekend rates and overtime, and estimates gross pay for each pay period.
