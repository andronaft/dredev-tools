R-454B Superheat & Subcooling Calculator

For air conditioners and heat pumps built for R-454B. It is a blend with temperature glide, so the calculator reads the dew point for superheat and the bubble point for subcooling.

Quick answer: R-454B has two saturation temperatures at one pressure. Superheat uses the dew point: 107 psig is 40.0 °F, so a 52 °F suction line has 12.0 °F of superheat. Subcooling uses the bubble point: 346 psig is 110.0 °F, so a 100 °F liquid line has 10.0 °F of subcooling.

What is particular about R-454B

A blend with about 2.3 °F of glide

R-454B is R-32 / R-1234yf, 68.9 / 31.1 % by weight (Chemours, Opteon™ XL41 (R-454B) product information (C-11001, 7/24)). Its components boil off at different rates, so at one pressure the liquid starts to boil at the bubble point and the last of it is gone at the dew point. On this chart the two are 2.3 °F apart at a 40 °F dew point and 2.3 °F apart at a 105 °F bubble point: at 107 psig the dew point is 40.0 °F and the bubble point 37.7 °F.

Chemours prints the rule in the key of its R-454B chart: saturated vapor pressure for superheat, saturated liquid pressure for subcooling (Chemours, Opteon™ XL41 (R-454B) Pressure-Temperature Guide for A/C). The Superheat and Subcooling buttons above switch the column for you. More on bubble and dew points.

Lower pressure than R-410A

Arkema lists R-454B as a replacement for R-410A in new equipment only (Arkema, 2025 Forane® Technical Digest). It runs somewhat lower: 107.0 psig against 118.8 psig as 40 °F vapor, and 322.8 psig against 341.9 psig as 105 °F liquid. An R-410A chart applied to a 107 psig suction reading gives 34.7 °F instead of 40.0 °F, which would overstate superheat by 5.3 °F. See R-454B vs R-410A.

A2L: what the manufacturers ask for

R-454B is in safety class A2L (lower toxicity, lower flammability) (ASHRAE Standard 34 class as listed by US EPA SNAP, Substitutes in Residential and Light Commercial Air Conditioning and Heat Pumps (page updated 19 March 2026)). Carrier's instructions call for non-sparking tools, a refrigerant detector before and during the work and no flame for leak checking (Carrier PH5SAN5 single-stage heat pump with R-454B, Installation Instructions). York requires service equipment rated for R-454B (York YH9/XH9 R-454B heat pump Installation Manual 6515371-UIM-B-0526), and Lennox notes that R-454B cylinders have a ¼″ left-hand flare connection (Lennox ML13KC1 Installation Instructions 508732-02 (03/2026)). It is charged as liquid only (Carrier, Residential Piping and Long Line Guideline for AC and heat pumps using R-454B), and Chemours states that A2L refrigerants are for new equipment only, not for retrofits.

Charging and GWP

R-454B units with a TXV or EEV are charged by subcooling to the value on the rating plate: how to charge an R-454B system by subcooling walks through the steps with the manufacturer documents. EPA gives R-454B a 100-year GWP of 465 (US EPA, Technology Transitions GWP Reference Table (page updated 2 April 2026)); Arkema names POE as the lubricant.

R-454B worked examples and pressures

The readings below are a comfort-cooling example. They show the arithmetic; they are not charging targets.

Worked example: R-454B superheat

  1. Suction (low-side) gauge pressure: 107 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 107 psig in the vapor (dew) column: 40.0 °F.
  3. Suction line temperature near the service valve: 52 °F.
  4. Superheat = 52 − 40.0 = 12.0 °F.

Result: 12.0 °F of superheat.

Worked example: R-454B subcooling

  1. Liquid (high-side) gauge pressure: 346 psig.
  2. Saturation temperature at 346 psig in the liquid (bubble) column: 110.0 °F.
  3. Liquid line temperature near the service valve: 100 °F.
  4. Subcooling = 110.0 − 100 = 10.0 °F.

Result: 10.0 °F of subcooling.

What should R-454B superheat and subcooling be?

There is no single correct number for a refrigerant. The target belongs to the equipment: a fixed-orifice (piston) system is charged to a target superheat from the manufacturer's charging table (target superheat calculator), and a TXV or EEV system to the target subcooling on the unit's data plate. Always use the manufacturer's numbers for the unit in front of you.

Questions

Which column do I use for R-454B, bubble or dew?

Dew (vapor) for superheat and bubble (liquid) for subcooling. At 107 psig they are 40.0 °F and 37.7 °F.

How much temperature glide does R-454B have?

About 2.3 °F on this chart: the dew point minus the bubble point at the same pressure, at a 40 °F dew point.

Can I use an R-410A PT chart for R-454B?

No. At 107 psig the R-410A chart reads 34.7 °F and the R-454B dew point is 40.0 °F, so superheat would come out 5.3 °F too high.

Is R-454B added as liquid or as vapor?

As liquid. It is a blend, and the equipment and refrigerant makers require it to leave the cylinder as liquid.

Where does the data come from?

Arkema Forane® Pressure Temperature Chart (PPC/SDC 05-2025). Between the printed 5 °F points the calculator interpolates along the vapor-pressure curve.
